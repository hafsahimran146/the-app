WOMEN'S WELLNESS

Every phase tells a story.

The menstrual cycle involves changing hormone levels throughout the month. Experiences, energy, appetite and symptoms can differ from person to person. Understanding your body can help you make informed choices about food, rest and movement.

🌸

01. Menstrual Phase

Menstruation begins when the uterine lining sheds. Rest when needed and choose balanced meals containing iron, protein and other important nutrients.

🌱

02. Follicular Phase

This phase starts on the first day of menstruation and continues towards ovulation. Hormone levels change as a follicle develops. Regular meals help support everyday energy.

🌼

03. Ovulation

An ovary releases an egg. Timing varies between people and cycles. A varied diet, hydration and sufficient sleep support general health.

🍂

04. Luteal Phase

Hormones change after ovulation. Some people notice changes in appetite, mood or energy. Regular balanced meals and snacks may help meet individual needs.

Important: These phases are educational, not a promise that everyone will experience the same symptoms or need different foods at each phase. Chocolate cannot treat menstrual disorders or guarantee hormone balance.

SEED-TO-SUSTAINABILITY

A little seed. A lasting idea.

Every Synced purchase is planned to include two to three seeds. Instead of ending with the snack, the experience can continue through planting, learning and caring for a growing plant.

🌰

1. Include Seeds

Provide suitable seeds in a clearly labelled packet, with planting instructions.

🌱

2. Plant & Grow

Encourage customers to plant suitable seeds and learn what each plant needs to thrive.

🌍

3. Give Back

Growing appropriate plants can support greener spaces and, depending on the species and location, provide habitat and food for wildlife.

Why do seeds matter?

Seeds are essential for plant reproduction and future growing. Some seeds lose viability over time or under poor storage conditions, but seeds do not all expire at the same rate. Synced's initiative aims to encourage planting and environmental awareness—not to claim that all seeds are being wasted.

We plan to choose suitable, viable seeds from responsible suppliers, consider local climate and native species, and avoid invasive plants. Seeds should be stored and packaged appropriately.
